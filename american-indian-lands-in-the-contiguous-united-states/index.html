American Indian Lands in the Contiguous United States

The geography of Indian Country in the U.S. is in constant flux. As tribes gain federal recognition, land is transferred from fee simple title to trust, and judicial decisions re-establish sovereignty... any map of American Indian land practically becomes outdated the moment it is produced. This map is a passion project that aims to capture the state of American Indian land in the lower 48 states as of April 2025. This map was presented at the 2026 National Tribal GIS Conference in Albuquerque, New Mexico
