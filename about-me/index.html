About Me

There’s never been a dull moment in my career as a Professional Geographer. I have worked in multiple tiers of local government, the non-profit world and the AEC consulting industry. My work has taken me to nine states (so far!) and several American Indian nations.

When I'm not immersed in databases, maps, and project management: I am a husband to my high-school sweetheart and father to one son (soon to be two!) . I am a lifelong resident of Jeffersonville, Indiana.
